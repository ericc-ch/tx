Los Angeles-based alternative pop/rock band LANY is set to return to Jakarta for their LANY: soft world tour on October 29 - 30, 2026 at Indonesia Arena. Known for their emotional anthems and captivating live performances, LANY has steadily carved out their place in the global music scene, led by the distinctive presence of frontman Paul Jason Klein.

Following the release of their latest albums Soft and Soft 2, the band continues to evolve with a more intimate yet expansive sound. With billions of streams, sold-out arena tours, and hit singles like ILYSB and Malibu Nights, this concert promises an unforgettable night filled with energy, emotion, and connection.
